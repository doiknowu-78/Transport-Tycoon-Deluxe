## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>
Transport Tycoon Deluxe is a complete free version of the beloved corporate strategy game that allows players to create their very own transport network. With all features included and continuous updates, you can immerse yourself in the world of transport management, ensuring every detail is just right.

Originally developed for MS-DOS, Transport Tycoon faced compatibility issues with newer Windows operating systems. MicroProse Software, Inc. recognized this and released Transport Tycoon Deluxe, a fully optimized version with enhanced graphics, improved gameplay, and a rich selection of scenarios that draw inspiration from both real and fictional worlds.

As the CEO of your transport company, you will manage various modes of transport including buses, trucks, trains, boats, and planes. Whether you're a newcomer or an experienced player, the tutorial mode will guide you through the initial stages of your business.
